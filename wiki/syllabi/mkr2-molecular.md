## 3. Study order (not the syllabus order)

The syllabus jumps around. Each block below builds on the previous one, so work through them in this order:

| Block | Pages | Why here | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Молекулярно-кінетична теорія** | 2 → 1 → 20–21 | the pressure and temperature of a gas from molecules; i, U = (i/2)νRT | 3 |
| **B. Статистичні розподіли** | 18 → 17,19 | Maxwell (speeds) → Boltzmann (potential field) → combined | 2 |
| **C. Явища переносу** | 22 → 23–25 | ⟨l⟩ first; then one shared pattern: D, η, λ ∝ ⅓⟨v⟩⟨l⟩ | 2 |
| **D. Перший закон** | 3 → 4 → 6 → 5 | U, Q, A → the first law → C_V, C_p, Mayer's equation | 4 |
| **E. Процеси** | 7–9 → 10 | a table: law, A, Q, ΔU, C for each process; then the adiabat | 2 |
| **F. Цикли і другий закон** | 11 → 12–13 → 16 → 14 → 15 | cycle → Carnot → 2nd law → entropy → 3rd law | 5 |
| **G. Реальні гази** | 26 → 27 | the deviations → the van der Waals equation and isotherms, critical state | 2 |
| **H. Конденсовані стани** | 28 → 29 → 30 | crystals → defects → liquids | 3 |

Concept pages to create along the way:
- `concepts/isoprocesses-table`: one table of all five processes. It's the most useful single page for the МКР.
- `concepts/transport-phenomena`: diffusion, viscosity and heat conduction as one law, "flux = −coefficient × gradient".
- `concepts/statistical-vs-thermodynamic`: two ways to describe the same gas (blocks A–C vs D–F).
