## [A] Закон Кулона (p. 22)
- The book's wording (worth learning by heart): «сила взаємодії F двох **нерухомих точкових зарядів у вакуумі** прямо пропорційна добуткові кількостей електрики цих зарядів q₁ і q₂, обернено пропорційна квадратові відстані r між ними і напрямлена вздовж прямої, яка сполучає ці заряди»
- $F = k\dfrac{q_1q_2}{r^2}$ (1.6) → in SI: $F = \dfrac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\dfrac{q_1q_2}{r^2}$ (1.7)
- **точковий заряд**: the size of the bodies ≪ the distance between them (p. 24).
- Sign convention: repulsion F > 0, attraction F < 0.
- ✏️ Sketch Coulomb's torsion balance (рис. 1.2): thread, beam, balls a and d.

## [B] Векторна форма (p. 24)
- $\vec F_{12} = \dfrac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\dfrac{q_1q_2}{r_{12}^3}\vec r_{12}$ (1.8), with $\vec F_{12} = -\vec F_{21}$ (1.9)
- The forces are **центральні** (central): they act along the line joining the charges.

## [C] Коефіцієнт і одиниці (pp. 23–24)
- $k = \dfrac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0} = 9\cdot10^9\ \dfrac{\text{Н·м}^2}{\text{Кл}^2}$; **електрична стала** $\varepsilon_0 = 8{,}85\cdot10^{-12}$ Ф/м.
- 1 Кл = 1 А·1 с; the ampere is the base unit, so the coulomb is a derived one.
- "Раціоналізована форма" puts the 4π in k so that it drops out of the formulas used most often.
- Optional: in СГСЕ k = 1, and 1 Кл = 3·10⁹ СГСЕ units.

## [D] Принцип суперпозиції (p. 25)
- The force between two charges **does not depend on other charges being present**; the net force is the vector sum:
  $\vec F = \sum_i \vec F_i = \sum_i \dfrac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\dfrac{q_iq_0}{r_{i0}^3}\vec r_{i0}$ (1.10)
- For extended bodies, divide them mentally into point charges.

## [E] Вплив середовища (§ 1.3, p. 26)
- **діелектрична проникність** $\varepsilon = F_0/F$, how many times weaker the force is in a medium than in vacuum (dimensionless).
- $F = \dfrac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\dfrac{q_1q_2}{\varepsilon r^2}$
- Why it happens: the medium undergoes **поляризація** (polarization) and partly neutralizes the charges.

## [F] Диполь у неоднорідному полі (p. 35)
- In a non-uniform field the two forces differ, so a net force appears as well as the torque:
  $F = F_1 - F_2 = qE - q\left(E - \dfrac{dE}{dx}l\right) = q\dfrac{dE}{dx}l$ (1.26), dipole along a field line (рис. 1.14).
- Since $p = ql$, this is $F = p\,\dfrac{dE}{dx}$. The book doesn't write that form; it is our shorthand.
- ⚠️ Book inconsistency: in the text $F_1 = qE$ acts on the charge in the stronger field (+q), but рис. 1.14 labels $F_1$ at −q. The result is correct.
- Direction: **towards increasing field strength**; the dipole is pulled into the stronger field.
- ✏️ Example to write: why a charged comb attracts scraps of paper (induced dipoles).
- Optional: Example 1.1, two dipoles on one line, $F = -\dfrac{6p_1p_2}{4\pi\varepsilon_0\varepsilon r^4}$.
- The definition of a dipole itself is in [topic 5](05_Диполь_в_однорідному_полі.md) (put it there or here, but only once).

## Footer: межі застосування (p. 26)
- Verified from about 10⁻¹⁷ м (electron scattering) up to about 10⁷ м (satellites).
