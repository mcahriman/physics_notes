# 7. Дивергенція вектора E — каркас

## [A] Мотивація (p. 41)
- The integral form links **different** points: E on the surface and q inside the volume.
- The differential form links quantities **at one point**, which fits close-range action.

## [B] Виведення (pp. 41–42)
- A tiny box dx·dy·dz around charge ρ dV. The flux through the pair of x-faces is $\dfrac{\partial E_x}{\partial x}dx\,dy\,dz$; the same goes for y and z.
- By Gauss: the total flux $\left(\dfrac{\partial E_x}{\partial x}+\dfrac{\partial E_y}{\partial y}+\dfrac{\partial E_z}{\partial z}\right)dx\,dy\,dz$ equals $\rho\,dx\,dy\,dz/\varepsilon_0$, so $\dfrac{\partial E_x}{\partial x}+\dfrac{\partial E_y}{\partial y}+\dfrac{\partial E_z}{\partial z} = \dfrac{\rho}{\varepsilon_0}$ (1.38)
- ✏️ Draw the box with E_x entering on the left and E_x + (∂E_x/∂x)dx leaving on the right.

## [C] Означення дивергенції (p. 42)
- **розходження, або дивергенція** of a vector A at a point is the limit of flux per unit volume:
  $\operatorname{div}\vec A = \lim\limits_{V\to0}\dfrac{\oint_S A_n\,dS}{V}$, which doesn't depend on the shape of S.
- Cartesian form: $\operatorname{div}\vec E = \dfrac{\partial E_x}{\partial x}+\dfrac{\partial E_y}{\partial y}+\dfrac{\partial E_z}{\partial z}$ (1.43)

## [D] Результат (pp. 42–43)
- $\operatorname{div}\vec E = \dfrac{\rho}{\varepsilon_0}$ (1.42), Gauss's theorem in differential form = **диференціальна форма закону Кулона**.
- Meaning: div measures the **потужність джерел або стоків** (strength of sources or sinks).
  - div E > 0: lines start here (+ charge).
  - div E < 0: lines end here (− charge).
  - Where ρ = 0: div E = 0, so the lines pass through.
