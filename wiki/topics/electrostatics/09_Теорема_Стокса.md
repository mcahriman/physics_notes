# 9. Теорема Стокса — каркас

├ [C] Декартова форма rot A (1.49)                                ┤
├ [D] Теорема Стокса: ∮A dl = ∫rot A dS                           ┤
└ [E] rot E = 0 (1.51); система div E = ρ/ε₀, rot E = 0 (1.52)    ┘

## [A] Циркуляція (p. 45)
- **циркуляція вектора E**: $\oint_L \vec E\,d\vec l$ around a closed loop L.
- Electrostatics: $\oint_L \vec E\,d\vec l = 0$ (1.46), the integral form of **потенціальність** (the field being conservative).
- Consequence: electrostatic field lines are **not closed**; they start and end on charges.

## [B] Ротор (p. 46)
- From the Latin *roto*, "I turn". It measures the «завихрення» (curl, or whirl) of a vector field.
- The projection onto the direction n: $\operatorname{rot}_n\vec A = \lim\limits_{\Delta S\to0}\dfrac{\oint_L \vec A\,d\vec l}{\Delta S}$ (1.47), with the loop direction set by the **правий гвинт** (right-hand screw) rule.

## [C] Декартова форма (p. 47)
$\operatorname{rot}\vec A = \left(\dfrac{\partial A_z}{\partial y}-\dfrac{\partial A_y}{\partial z}\right)\vec i + \left(\dfrac{\partial A_x}{\partial z}-\dfrac{\partial A_z}{\partial x}\right)\vec j + \left(\dfrac{\partial A_y}{\partial x}-\dfrac{\partial A_x}{\partial y}\right)\vec k$ (1.49)

## [D] Теорема Стокса (p. 47)
- $\oint_L \vec A\,d\vec l = \int_S \operatorname{rot}\vec A\,d\vec S$, where S is **any** surface bounded by the loop L.
- Words: the circulation around a loop = the flux of the curl through a surface spanning that loop.
- ✏️ Sketch: a loop L with a "soap film" S on it and normals n.

## [E] Застосування до E (pp. 47–48)
- $\int_S \operatorname{rot}\vec E\,d\vec S = \oint \vec E\,d\vec l = 0$ (1.50) for any S, so $\operatorname{rot}\vec E = 0$ (1.51).
- The two **basic differential equations of electrostatics** (1.52):
  $\operatorname{div}\vec E = \rho/\varepsilon_0,\qquad \operatorname{rot}\vec E = 0$
- These replace Coulomb's law plus superposition, and they are a special case of **Maxwell's equations**.

## Addendum (not in the book)
Our shorthand and derivations. The book doesn't print these forms.
- **Memory aid for (1.49)** (→ [C]): rot A is a 3×3 determinant with rows (i j k), (∂/∂x ∂/∂y ∂/∂z), (A_x A_y A_z). Modern notation writes rot A as ∇ × A and div A as ∇ · A.
