# 17. Енергія зарядженого провідника — каркас

[← 16_Зєднання_конденсаторів](16_Зєднання_конденсаторів.md) · [index](../../index.md) · [syllabus](../../syllabi/electrostatics.md) · [glossary](../../glossary.md) · [18_Енергія_електростатичного_поля →](18_Енергія_електростатичного_поля.md)

📖 т. 2, **§ 1.25** «Енергія взаємодії електричних зарядів» (с. 101–103), **§ 1.26** start (с. 103–104). ✅ Formulas checked against the scan.

## Layout
```
┌ [A] Енергія двох зарядів (1.128)–(1.129) ┐
├ [B] Система n зарядів (1.130): чому ½    ┤
├ [C] Заряджання провідника: dA = φ dq → (1.132) ┤
└ [D] Те саме для конденсатора (1.133)       ┘
```

## [A] Два точкові заряди (pp. 101–102)
- Bring the charges from infinity: $W = \dfrac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\dfrac{q_1q_2}{r_{12}}$ (1.128)
- In symmetric form: $W = \tfrac12(q_1\varphi_1 + q_2\varphi_2)$ (1.129), where φ₁ is the potential that q₂ creates at q₁.

## [B] Система n зарядів (p. 102)
- $W = \dfrac12\sum_{i=1}^n q_i\varphi_i$ (1.130), where φᵢ is the potential at qᵢ from **all the other** charges.
- **Why ½**: each pair is counted twice in the sum.
- Continuous charges: $W = \tfrac12\left(\int\rho\varphi\,dV + \int\sigma\varphi\,dS\right)$.

## [C] Енергія зарядженого провідника (pp. 103–104)
- Charge the conductor in portions dq brought from infinity. Each costs $dA = \varphi\,dq = \dfrac{q}{C}dq$.
- From 0 to q: $A = \dfrac{q^2}{2C}$, which becomes the stored energy.
- $W = \dfrac{q\varphi}{2} = \dfrac{C\varphi^2}{2} = \dfrac{q^2}{2C}$ (1.132)

## [D] Конденсатор (p. 104)
- (1.132) also gives the energy of a capacitor, with φ replaced by the voltage Δφ = U. For a parallel-plate capacitor: $W_п = \tfrac12C\Delta\varphi^2$ (1.133)
- Picture: carry charge from one plate to the other.

## Addendum (not in the book)
Our shorthand and derivations. The book doesn't print these forms.
- **Where the ½ comes from** (→ [C]): φ = q/C is a straight line through 0 on a φ–q graph, and the work $\int\varphi\,dq$ is the area under it, a triangle. ✏️ Worth a small sketch.
- **All three forms for a capacitor** (→ [D]): $W = \dfrac{CU^2}{2} = \dfrac{qU}{2} = \dfrac{q^2}{2C}$, from (1.132) with U = Δφ.
- **Notation** (→ all): in §§ 1.25–1.26 the book writes potential energy as $W_п$. It uses W in § 1.6 and U in § 1.11 for the same kind of quantity.
