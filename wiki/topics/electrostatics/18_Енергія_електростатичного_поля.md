# 18. Енергія електростатичного поля — каркас

## [A] Перетворення для плоского конденсатора (p. 104)
- Start from $W = \tfrac12 C(\Delta\varphi)^2$ and put in $\Delta\varphi = Ed$ and $C = \dfrac{\varepsilon_0\varepsilon S}{d}$:
- $W = \dfrac{\varepsilon_0\varepsilon E^2}{2}Sd = \dfrac{\varepsilon_0\varepsilon E^2}{2}V$ (1.134), where V = Sd is the volume between the plates.
- The energy is now written through the **field E** and the **volume**, with no charges in it.

## [B] Загальний випадок (p. 104)
- A non-uniform field: $W = \dfrac12\int_V \varepsilon_0\varepsilon E^2\,dV$ (1.135)

## [C] Густина енергії (p. 105)
- **об'ємна густина енергії електричного поля**:
  $w = \dfrac{W}{V} = \dfrac{\varepsilon_0\varepsilon E^2}{2} = \dfrac{ED}{2}$ (1.136)
- Here $D = \varepsilon_0\varepsilon E$ is the **вектор електричної індукції** (electric displacement), defined in the dielectrics sections, §1.16+. One line on the page is enough.
- Unit: Дж/м³.

## [D] Фізична інтерпретація (p. 105), worth a boxed note
- **Близькодія**: the energy is carried by the **field** and spread through space with density w.
- **Далекодія**: the energy sits on the charged conductors.
- In **electrostatics no experiment can tell them apart**, because the field can't be separated from its charges.
- **Changing fields** settle it: EM waves carry energy with no charges present, so the energy is in the field.
