## [A] Електричний опір (pp. 111–112)
- $R = 1/k$, the inverse of conductance. 1 Ом: 1 А flows at 1 В.
- Where it comes from (metals): electrons **scatter** on lattice defects and **thermal vibrations** of the lattice.
- It doesn't depend on I (for moderate currents); it depends on geometry, material and state (T, p).

## [B] Опір провідника (pp. 112–113)
- $R = \rho\dfrac{l}{S}$ (2.18), where ρ is the **питомий опір** (resistivity), in Ом·м.
- $\sigma = 1/\rho$ (2.21), the **питома електропровідність** (conductivity), in См/м.
- Lowest ρ: silver, copper, gold, aluminium (used for wires). High ρ: nichrome (heaters), constantan and nickeline (reference resistors).
- Classification by σ: провідники σ > 10⁶, діелектрики σ < 10⁻¹⁰, напівпровідники in between (См/м); checked against the scan.

## [C] Температурна залежність (pp. 112–113)
- The **температурний коефіцієнт опору** $\alpha = \dfrac1\rho\dfrac{d\rho}{dT}$ (2.19). Metals: α > 0. Electrolytes: α < 0.
- For metals near 0 °C, $\alpha\approx 1/273$ К⁻¹ and $\rho = \rho_0(1+\alpha t)$, or $\rho = \rho_0\alpha T$ (2.20).
- Uses: resistance thermometers, thermostats, bolometers.

## [D] Робота струму (p. 121)
- $A = IUt$ (2.38) $= I^2Rt = \dfrac{U^2}{R}t$ (2.39)
- The whole source over the whole loop: $A_{заг} = \mathcal E It$ (2.41).

## [E] Потужність (pp. 121–123)
- **Потужність** (power) is the work per unit time.
- **Корисна** (useful, in the load R): $P = IU = I^2R = \dfrac{U^2}{R}$ (2.43) $= \dfrac{\mathcal E^2R}{(R+r)^2}$ (2.44)
- **Загальна** (total): $P_{дж} = \mathcal EI = \dfrac{\mathcal E^2}{R+r}$ (2.45)
- **ККД** (efficiency): $\eta = \dfrac{P}{P_{дж}} = \dfrac{R}{R+r}$ (2.46)
- Maximum useful power when **R = r**: $P_{max} = \dfrac{\mathcal E^2}{4r}$, but then η = 50 %.
- ✏️ Graph P(R): zero at R = 0, a peak at R = r, tending to 0 as R → ∞. Overlay η(R) rising towards 1.

## Addendum (not in the book)
Our shorthand and derivations. The book doesn't print these forms.
- **Notation** (→ [E]): the book's $P_{дж}$ is the total power of the source (дж = джерело). It also writes $A_{заг}$ for the total work (2.41). The ККД is η here, the same letter as viscosity in vol. 1 and linear charge density in §1.7 examples.
