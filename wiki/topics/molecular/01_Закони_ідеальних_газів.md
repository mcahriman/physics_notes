## [A] Рівняння стану ідеального газу (pp. 311–313)
- **Термічне рівняння стану** (thermal equation of state): an equation that links p, V, T at thermal equilibrium, $F(p, V, T) = 0$ (14.12).
- From the MKT: $p = n_0kT$ (14.13) ⟹ $pV = nkT$ (14.14), where n is the number of molecules.
- For one mole: $pV_M = N_AkT$ (14.15). With **молярна газова стала** $R = kN_A = 8{,}31451$ Дж/(моль·К):
  $pV_M = RT$ (14.16), the **рівняння Клапейрона**.
- For ν moles: $pV = \nu RT$ (14.17). Since ν = m/M = n/N_A:
  $$pV = \frac mM RT,\quad\text{або}\quad pV = \frac{n}{N_A}RT\qquad(14.18)$$
  the **рівняння Клапейрона–Менделєєва**.
- Meaning of R (p. 313): the work done by 1 mole of ideal gas expanding **isobarically** when heated by 1 К. ✏️ Sketch the piston of рис. 14.4.

## [B] Ізопроцеси (m = const) (pp. 313–316)
| процес | умова | закон (book's name) | рівняння | графік |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ізотермічний** | T = const | закон **Бойля–Маріотта** | pV = const (14.22) | p–V: hyperbolas (рис. 14.5) |
| **ізобарний** | p = const | закон **Гей-Люссака** | V/T = const (14.25); V = V₀(1 + αt) (14.26) | V–T: straight lines to the origin (рис. 14.6) |
| **ізохорний** | V = const | "рівняння ізохорного процесу" | p/T = const (14.28); p = p₀(1 + βt) (14.29) | p–T: straight lines to the origin (рис. 14.7) |

- α = β = 0,003661 К⁻¹ ≈ 1/273, the **коефіцієнт об'ємного розширення** and the **термічний коефіцієнт тиску**.
- Optional: the isothermal compressibility $\chi = -\dfrac1V\left(\dfrac{\partial V}{\partial p}\right)_T = \dfrac1p$ (14.24), and the link $\beta = \alpha/(p\chi)$ (14.31).
- In the textbook the ізохорний law is **not named after Charles**. If your lecturer uses "закон Шарля", add it yourself.
- ✏️ Draw the three small graphs with several curves each (T₁ < T₂, p₁ < p₂, V₁ < V₂), and mark which curve has the higher parameter.
- This page has the laws only. Work, heat and ΔU for each process come in block E ([07–09](07-09_Ізопроцеси.md)).

## [C] Закон Авогадро (p. 316)
- From pV = nkT: «в однакових об'ємах ідеальних газів при однакових температурах і тисках міститься однакове число молекул»

## [D] Межі застосування (p. 313)
- The model holds when the molecules' own volume and their interaction can be neglected: **low pressure and high enough temperature**. Normal atmospheric conditions mostly qualify. Leads to [26 реальний газ](26_Реальний_газ.md).

## Optional (Example 14.1, p. 316)
- Derive pV/T = const from experiment alone: isotherm 1→1′, then isochore 1′→2. A good exam trick, and it's half a page.

## Addendum (not in the book)
Our shorthand and derivations. The book doesn't print these forms.
- **Notation: n, n₀, ν, V_M** (→ [A]): the book writes the number of molecules as **n** and the concentration (molecules per m³) as **n₀** = n/V. Many English texts use N for the number and n for the concentration, so the same letter n means different things. ν is the amount in moles, M the molar mass (older texts: μ), V_M the molar volume. The book also mentions the specific gas constant B, with pV = mBT (Example 14.1).
