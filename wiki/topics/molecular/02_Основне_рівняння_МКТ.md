## [A] Ідеальний газ (pp. 299–300)
- The book's definition (p. 300): we can neglect the size of the molecules («вважати молекули матеріальними точками») and their interaction at a distance, counting only collisions. «Такий газ називають **ідеальним**».
- **Основні положення МКТ газів** (6 of them, p. 300). Copy them as a short list:
  1. The molecules are identical spheres of negligible size, with no attraction or repulsion between them.
  2. Even a small volume holds very many molecules, so statistical laws apply.
  3. Collisions are **elastic**.
  4. The time between collisions ≫ the time of a collision, so between collisions the motion is uniform and straight.
  5. With no external forces the molecules are spread evenly through the volume.
  6. All directions of velocity are equally likely, the **«молекулярний хаос»**.
- **Тепловий рух** (thermal motion): the completely chaotic motion of molecules (p. 300).

## [B] Виведення (pp. 301–303)
- Cube of edge l (рис. 14.1). One molecule hits the right wall: Δp = 2m₀v_x. It returns after Δt = 2l/v_x, so the average force is $f = m_0v_x^2/l$.
- Sum over all n molecules and divide by S = l². The directions are equally likely, so p_x = p_y = p_z, which gives
  $p = \dfrac23\dfrac1{l^3}\sum_{i=1}^n\dfrac{m_{0i}v_i^2}{2}$ (14.5)
- **Середня квадратична швидкість** (root-mean-square speed): $\langle v_{кв}^2\rangle = \dfrac{v_1^2+v_2^2+\ldots+v_n^2}{n}$
- ✏️ Sketch: a cube, one molecule bouncing off the right face, with arrows v_x → and −v_x ←.

## [C] Основне рівняння МКТ (p. 303) — put it in a box
$$p = \frac23\,n_0\,\frac{m_0\langle v_{кв}^2\rangle}{2}\qquad(14.6)$$
- n₀ = n/V is the **number of molecules per unit volume**; $m_0\langle v_{кв}^2\rangle/2$ is the **average kinetic energy of translational motion of one molecule**.
- In words (p. 303): «тиск ідеального газу дорівнює двом третинам середньої кінетичної енергії поступального руху молекул одиниці об'єму газу»
- Why it matters: it links **microscopic** quantities (m₀, v) to a **macroscopic** one (p) that can be measured.
- Pressure is a **статистичний параметр** (statistical parameter): it makes no sense for a single molecule (p. 304).
- Units: Па = Н/м². Also 1 атм = 101 325 Па; 1 тор = 133,322 Па; 1 бар = 10⁵ Па.

## [D] Температура (pp. 305–308)
- **Термодинамічна рівновага** (thermodynamic equilibrium): all macroscopic changes stop. **Релаксація** (relaxation) is the spontaneous approach to it; the **час релаксації** is how long it takes. Its existence is the **нульове начало термодинаміки** (zeroth law), p. 306.
- The piston example (рис. 14.2): at equilibrium the average kinetic energies on both sides even out. So temperature "behaves like" ⟨ε⟩, and it is an **інтенсивний параметр** (it doesn't depend on the mass of the system).
- Definition in energy units: $\Theta = \dfrac23\dfrac{m_0\langle v_{кв}^2\rangle}{2}$, which gives $p = n_0\Theta$ (14.8)
- In kelvins: $\Theta = kT$ (14.9), where k is the **стала Больцмана** (Boltzmann constant), 1,38·10⁻²³ Дж/К.
- $$\frac{m_0\langle v_{кв}^2\rangle}{2} = \frac32kT\qquad(14.10)$$
- Meaning of k: by how much the energy of translational motion of one molecule changes when T changes by 1 К.

## [E] Наслідок (p. 311)
- Combining (14.6) and (14.10): $p = n_0kT$ (14.13). This is the step to [01 gas laws](01_Закони_ідеальних_газів.md).
