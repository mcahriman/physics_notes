## [A] Рівняння Майєра (pp. 382–383) — put it in a box
- From the first law for one mole, $d'Q = C_VdT + p\,dV$ (16.17):
  - V = const: dV = 0, so $C_V = \left(\dfrac{\partial U}{\partial T}\right)_V$ (16.16)
  - p = const: p dV = R dT, so $d'Q = C_VdT + R\,dT$ (16.18)
  $$C_p = C_V + R\qquad(16.19)$$
  the **рівняння Майєра** (Mayer's equation).
- Meaning: at constant p, part of the heat goes into **work done by expansion**, not only into U. So C_p > C_V, and by exactly R (the meaning of R from [01](01_Закони_ідеальних_газів.md)).

## [B] Класична теорія (pp. 390–392)
- The book gives C_V and C_p for each kind of gas (p. 392); the γ column is our C_p/C_V.
| газ | i | C_V | C_p | γ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| одноатомний | 3 | 3/2 R | 5/2 R | 5/3 ≈ 1,67 |
| двохатомний (жорсткий) | 5 | 5/2 R | 7/2 R | 7/5 = 1,4 |
| багатоатомний (жорсткий) | 6 | 3R | 4R | 4/3 ≈ 1,33 |
- γ is the **показник адіабати** (adiabatic index); it is needed for topic 10.
- The general formula with vibrations: $U = (3 + r + 2s)\dfrac{RT}2$ (16.46).

## [C] Невдачі класичної теорії (pp. 392–394)
1. Complex molecules: for C₂H₅OH the rigid model gives 3R and the elastic one 24R, but experiment gives about 8R. **Both models fail.**
2. **The temperature dependence** isn't explained. For a diatomic gas, рис. 16.7:
   - low T: C_V ≈ 3/2 R, as if there were translation only (H₂ below 60 К behaves as monatomic);
   - room T: 5/2 R (rotation "switched on");
   - thousands of К: towards 7/2 R (vibrations switch on, but molecules dissociate first).
- ✏️ Draw the "staircase" C_V(T) with steps at T_оберт and T_кол.

## [D] Квантове пояснення (pp. 394–397), short
- The energies of rotation and vibration are **quantized**: $\varepsilon_{пост} \ll \varepsilon_{оберт} \ll \varepsilon_{кол}$, hence the **характеристичні температури** (characteristic temperatures) $T_{пост} \ll T_{оберт} \ll T_{кол}$.
- Below T_оберт or T_кол, kT is too small to excite that motion, so its degrees of freedom are "frozen".
- Values: T_оберт for H₂ is 85 К, for N₂ 2,85 К; T_кол for N₂ is 3340 К, for O₂ 2240 К.

## Addendum (not in the book)
Our shorthand and derivations. The book doesn't print these forms.
- **General formulas** (→ [B]): from U = (i/2)RT, $C_V = \dfrac i2R$, $C_p = \dfrac{i+2}2R$, $\gamma = \dfrac{C_p}{C_V} = \dfrac{i+2}i$. They reproduce the book's table for i = 3, 5, 6.
- **Subscripts** (→ [D]): the book writes vibrational quantities with «колив» in (16.4) but «кол» in § 16.5 ($T_{кол}$, $\varepsilon_{кол}$). English texts use θ_rot, θ_vib (or Θ_r, Θ_v) for the characteristic temperatures.
