## [A] Цикл Карно (p. 399)
- Carnot's 1824 questions: is there a limit to improving heat engines, and does the working substance matter?
- «Він складається з **двох адіабатних та двох ізотермічних** процесів». It is **reversible**: the cycle of an **ideal** heat engine, with no losses to conduction, radiation or friction.
- It is also the route to a quantitative **second law** ([16](16_Другий_закон_ТД.md)).

## [B] Прямий цикл з ідеальним газом (pp. 400–401), 1 mole, clockwise
| стадія | процес | контакт | робота | теплота |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1→2 | ізотермічне розширення, T₁ | **нагрівник** (heater) | $A_{12} = RT_1\ln\frac{V_2}{V_1} = Q_1$ (16.55) | Q₁ > 0 received |
| 2→3 | адіабатне розширення | isolated | $A_{23} = C_V(T_1 - T_2)$ (16.56) | 0 |
| 3→4 | ізотермічне стискання, T₂ | **холодильник** (cooler) | $A_{34} = -RT_2\ln\frac{V_3}{V_4} = -Q_2$ (16.57) | Q₂ given away |
| 4→1 | адіабатне стискання | isolated | $A_{41} = C_V(T_2 - T_1)$ (16.58) | 0 |
- The adiabatic works cancel. From the adiabats: $\dfrac{V_2}{V_1} = \dfrac{V_3}{V_4}$ (16.63), the **condition for the cycle to close**.
- ✏️ The main drawing, рис. 16.9: two isotherms (T₁ upper, T₂ lower) and two steeper adiabats, with arrows 1→2→3→4 and Q₁ in, Q₂ out.

## [C] ККД (pp. 400–401) — put it in a box
$$\eta = \frac A{Q_1} = \frac{Q_1 - Q_2}{Q_1} = 1 - \frac{Q_2}{Q_1}\ (16.53)\qquad \eta = \frac{T_1 - T_2}{T_1} = 1 - \frac{T_2}{T_1}\ (16.64)$$
- The efficiency of a reversible Carnot cycle depends **only on the temperatures of the heater and the cooler**.
- **η < 1 always**: η = 1 would need T₁ → ∞ or T₂ → 0, which is impossible.
- If ΔT → 0 then η → 0, so a heat engine **must have two thermostats**.

## [D] Обернений цикл (pp. 399, 402–403)
- It runs anticlockwise: the work of **external forces** carries heat from the **colder** body to the **hotter** one.
- **Холодильна машина** (refrigerator): $\varepsilon = \dfrac{Q_2}{A}$ (16.68), the **холодильний коефіцієнт**, heat taken from the cold body per unit of work.
- **Тепловий насос** (heat pump): $\varphi = \dfrac{Q_1}{A} = \dfrac{T_1}{T_1 - T_2}$ (16.67), the **коефіцієнт перетворення**, heat delivered to the warm body per unit of work. It can be > 1.

## [E] Три типи теплових машин (p. 402)
- ✏️ Three small block diagrams (рис. 16.10–16.12):
  - двигун (engine): heater → Q₁ → working body → A out, and Q₂ → cooler;
  - холодильник (refrigerator): A in, cold body → Q₂ → working body → Q₁ → surroundings;
  - тепловий насос (heat pump): the same arrows, but the goal is Q₁ going into the warm room.

## Addendum (not in the book)
Our shorthand and derivations. The book doesn't print these forms.
- **Refrigerator on a reverse Carnot cycle** (→ [D]): from (16.64), $\varepsilon = \dfrac{T_2}{T_1 - T_2}$. Note φ = ε + 1, since Q₁ = Q₂ + A.
- **Sign of Q₂** (→ [B], [C]): on p. 400 the book says the gas gives the cooler «Q₂ (Q₂ < 0)», yet (16.53) and (16.57) use Q₂ as a positive amount, and (16.65) writes |Q₂|. In § 16.8 (topic 16) Q₂ is signed again. Rule of thumb: in η = 1 − Q₂/Q₁, Q₂ is the **magnitude** of the heat given away; in Clausius's Q₁/T₁ + Q₂/T₂ ≤ 0 it is **signed** (negative).
- **Names of the coefficients** (→ [D]): the book's холодильний коефіцієнт ε is the English coefficient of performance COP_R; its коефіцієнт перетворення φ is COP_HP. The book's thermal efficiency is η or $\eta_т$.
