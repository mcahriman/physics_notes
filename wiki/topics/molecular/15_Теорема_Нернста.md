# 15. Теорема Нернста — каркас

## [A] Навіщо (p. 414)
- The first and second laws give only **changes** in entropy; S₀ in (16.76) stays undetermined. That's enough until the **chemical composition** changes, which is the problem of chemical affinity.
- So a **new postulate** was needed, generalizing new experiments at low temperatures.

## [B] Формулювання Нернста (1906) (pp. 414–415)
- «при наближенні температури до абсолютного нуля ентропія будь-якої рівноважної системи при ізотермічних процесах **перестає залежати від термодинамічних параметрів стану** і при T → 0 набуває **ту саму для всіх систем сталу величину**, яку можна вважати такою, що дорівнює нулю»
- Nernst considered only **condensed** systems: solids and liquids.

## [C] Формулювання Планка (p. 415)
- Planck went further: entropy itself goes to zero, for **any** system:
  $$\lim_{T\to0}S = 0\qquad(16.93)$$
  the most general statement of the third law.

## [D] Наслідки (p. 415)
1. **Absolute zero can't be reached**. All cooling methods use adiabatic processes. Near 0 К the difference between adiabatic and isothermal processes vanishes, so cooling becomes ever less effective. You can only approach 0 К asymptotically. The law is sometimes stated as the «принцип недосяжності абсолютного нуля».
2. $C_p, C_V \to 0$ as T → 0. This is confirmed by experiment and by the quantum theories of Einstein and Debye; compare [05](05_Теплоємність_ідеальних_газів.md), where classical theory fails.
3. The thermal coefficients of expansion and pressure → 0.

## The three laws together (good for the page footer)
| закон | суть |
|---|---|
| I | energy is conserved: Q = ΔU + A |
| II | direction: S of an isolated system doesn't decrease |
| III | S → 0 as T → 0; 0 К can't be reached |
