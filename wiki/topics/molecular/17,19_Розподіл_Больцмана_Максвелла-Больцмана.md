## [A] Барометрична формула (pp. 331–333)
- Only the combined action of **thermal motion + gravity** gives the atmosphere its shape. Without gravity the molecules would spread out; without thermal motion they would fall to the ground.
- A thin layer dh: $dp = -\rho g\,dh$ (14.64), with ρ = m₀n₀ and n₀ = p/kT ⟹ $\dfrac{dp}{p} = -\dfrac{m_0g}{kT}dh$ (14.66)
- Integrate:
  $$p = p_0e^{-\frac{m_0g(h-h_0)}{kT}}\ (14.67),\qquad p = p_0e^{-\frac{Mg(h-h_0)}{RT}}\ (14.68)$$
  the **барометрична формула** (Laplace, 1821).
- Assumptions: an ideal gas, **T = const**, **g = const**, so it is accurate only up to a few km.
- The pressure falls exponentially, and faster for heavier gases (larger M) and colder ones (smaller T).
- ✏️ Sketch рис. 14.16: a column of air, a layer dh, p and p − dp.

## [B] Розподіл Больцмана (p. 335) — put it in a box
- Since p = nkT: $n = n_0e^{-\frac{m_0g(h-h_0)}{kT}}$ (14.70)
- General form:
  $$n = n_0e^{-\frac{\Delta U}{kT}}\qquad(14.71)$$
  where ΔU is the difference in a molecule's **potential energy** (ΔU = m₀gh in gravity).
- Boltzmann showed it is **universal**: it holds for any particles in thermal equilibrium in **any potential field**. «Цей статистичний закон називається **розподілом Больцмана**»
- Meaning: the concentration is higher where U is lower, which is the tendency towards stable equilibrium.
- In a layer: $\dfrac{dn}{n} = e^{-\frac{m_0g}{kT}dh}$ (14.73). ⚠️ Copied as printed on p. 336, but it can't be right: dh sits inside the exponent. The fraction of molecules in the layer h…h + dh is $\dfrac{dn}{n} = \dfrac{m_0g}{kT}e^{-\frac{m_0gh}{kT}}dh$.
- ✏️ Graph рис. 14.18: n against h, an exponential decay from n₀.

## [C] Розподіл Максвелла–Больцмана (p. 336)
- Substitute n from (14.71) into Maxwell's (14.57):
  $$dn = \frac4{\sqrt\pi}n_0\left(\frac{m_0}{2kT}\right)^{3/2}e^{-\frac{m_0\Delta\varphi}{kT}}e^{-\frac{m_0v^2}{2kT}}v^2\,dv\qquad(14.74)$$
  where φ = gh is the potential.
- **Two independent factors**: the Boltzmann factor $e^{-m_0\Delta\varphi/kT}$ (position) × the Maxwell factor $e^{-m_0v^2/2kT}$ (speed).
- So the Boltzmann distribution doesn't depend on the speed distribution, and Maxwell's doesn't depend on the potential.
- ✏️ Memory aid: $e^{-(E_п + E_к)/kT}$, energy divided by kT in the exponent.

## [D] Optional
- Planets' atmospheres (pp. 333–334): only molecules in the "tail" of Maxwell's distribution exceed escape velocity, so light planets (the Moon) lose their atmosphere.
- Fluctuations (Smoluchowski, p. 335): $\delta = \sqrt{\dfrac2\pi\dfrac1n}$ (14.72), smaller when there are more particles.
- Perrin's experiment (§ 14.12) used Boltzmann's law to measure N_A.
