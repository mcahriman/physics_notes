## [A] Навіщо (p. 343)
- Molecules move at hundreds of м/с, but along a **zigzag**, so they don't get far. Collisions set up equilibrium and the Maxwell distribution.
- **Середня довжина вільного пробігу** ⟨l⟩: «середню відстань, яку проходить молекула між двома послідовними зіткненнями»
- The model: molecules are identical, perfectly elastic spheres of diameter d; only collisions between **pairs** count.

## [B] Виведення (pp. 343–344)
- Freeze all the molecules but one. In 1 с it sweeps out a cylinder of base πd² and length ⟨v⟩, and hits every molecule whose centre lies inside: ⟨z⟩ = πd²n₀⟨v⟩.
- The others move too, so use the relative speed $\langle v\rangle_{відн} = \sqrt2\langle v\rangle$ (from Maxwell's distribution):
  $$\langle z\rangle = \sqrt2\,\pi d^2n_0\langle v\rangle\qquad(15.1)$$
  $$\langle l\rangle = \frac{\langle v\rangle}{\langle z\rangle} = \frac{1}{\sqrt2\,\pi d^2n_0}\qquad(15.2)$$
- ✏️ Sketch рис. 15.1: a molecule, a "straightened" cylinder of radius d, and molecules whose centres are inside it.

## [C] Ефективний переріз (pp. 344–345)
- **Повний ефективний поперечний переріз** σ = πd². It is measured in m² but has no geometric meaning: it describes the **probability** of the collision process.
- The **ефективний діаметр** shrinks as T rises (Sutherland): $d^2 = d_0^2(1 + C/T)$ (15.3), so ⟨l⟩ grows with T.

## [D] Порядки величин і залежності (p. 345)
- N₂ at normal conditions: ⟨l⟩ ≈ 0,6·10⁻⁷ м, about **1000 times the size of a molecule**. Collisions are rare even at atmospheric pressure.
- At T = const, **⟨l⟩ ∝ 1/p**. When ⟨l⟩ reaches the size of the container the gas is in a **вакуум** state (§ 15.5).

## [E] Послаблення пучка (pp. 345–347), optional
- $-\dfrac{dn}{n} = \dfrac{dx}{\langle l\rangle}$ (15.4) ⟹ $n = n_0e^{-x/\langle l\rangle}$ (15.5). Only about 1/e ≈ ⅓ of molecules travel further than ⟨l⟩.
- Born's experiment (1921): a beam of silver atoms through four diaphragms gives ⟨l⟩ directly (15.9).
