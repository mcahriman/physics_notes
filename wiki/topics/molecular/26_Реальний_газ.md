## [A] Реальний газ (p. 419)
- «**Реальними** називають гази, для яких суттєву роль відіграють міжмолекулярні сили взаємодії»
- Compared with the ideal model (non-interacting point molecules), two things are added:
  1. **the molecules' own volume**. At normal conditions it is about 10⁻⁴ of the gas volume, but at about 10⁸ Па it is significant;
  2. **intermolecular attraction**, which adds extra pressure.

## [B] Характер відхилень (pp. 419–420)
- **Кількісні** (quantitative): the ideal-gas laws, pV = RT included, hold only approximately.
- **Якісні** (qualitative): interaction lets a gas turn into a **liquid and a solid**, which is impossible for an ideal gas.
- The ideal-gas laws hold at **low pressure and high temperature**, when the average potential energy of interaction ≪ the average kinetic energy.

## [C] Сили міжмолекулярної взаємодії (pp. 420–421)
- Repulsion $F_1 \propto 1/r^n$ (n = 9–15) acts at short range; attraction $F_2 \propto 1/r^m$ (m = 7) acts further out.
- At r = r₀, F = 0, the **рівноважна відстань** (equilibrium distance). For r > r₀ attraction wins; for r < r₀ repulsion wins.
- ✏️ The main drawing, рис. 17.2: F(r), with the repulsion curve above, the attraction curve below, their sum and the zero at r₀. Optionally add the potential energy curve with a well at r₀.

## [D] Сили Ван дер Ваальса (pp. 421–422)
- Weak forces of attraction at distances of about 10⁻⁹ м.
| тип | автор | у яких молекул | природа | особливості |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| орієнтаційні | Кеєзом | polar (H₂O, NH₃) | permanent dipoles | depend on T, not additive |
| індукційні | Дебай | polar + induced | induced dipole | small, don't depend on T |
| дисперсійні | Лондон | **non-polar** (the main ones) | instantaneous dipoles from electron motion | additive |
- **Водневий зв'язок** (hydrogen bond), p. 422: specific attraction in water and alcohols through OH groups.

## [E] Optional: ізотерми Амага (p. 420)
- In pV–p coordinates an ideal gas gives straight lines; a real gas's curves first dip (attraction) and then rise (own volume). The minimum lies on the **крива Бойля**; T_Б is the **температура Бойля**.
