## [A] Рівняння (p. 423) — put it in a box
$$\left(p + \frac a{V_M^2}\right)(V_M - b) = RT\qquad(17.4)$$
- For one mole. V_M is the molar volume; **a, b** are experimental constants of the gas.
- **a/V_M²** accounts for the **attraction** of molecules. It has the dimension of pressure and is called the **внутрішній тиск** (internal pressure), p_i.
- **b** corrects for the molecules' **own volume** (repulsion at short range).
- For mass m (17.7): $\left(p + \dfrac{m^2}{M^2}\dfrac a{V^2}\right)\left(V - \dfrac mMb\right) = \dfrac mMRT$
- It is only approximate: quantitatively good at high T and low p, but **qualitatively** valid everywhere.

## [B] Звідки поправки (pp. 423–424)
- **b**: the centres of two hard spheres of radius r can't come closer than 2r, so the "forbidden" volume per molecule is ½·(4/3)π(2r)³ = 4·(4/3)πr³. So **b = 4 × the volume of the molecules** in a mole.
- **a/V²**: a molecule near the wall is pulled back into the gas. That force ∝ n₀ and the number of molecules hitting the wall ∝ n₀, so p_i ∝ n₀² ∝ 1/V².
- ✏️ Sketch a molecule near the wall with the forces from its neighbours pulling it inwards.

## [C] Ізотерми (pp. 425–430)
- Multiplying out gives a **cubic in V_M** (17.8): for given p and T there can be **three** volumes.
- Below T_к: an S-shaped "wave". On the **experimental** isotherm it is replaced by a **horizontal** line (рис. 17.5), where liquid and vapour coexist at constant p.
  - The right branch is gas; the left, steep branch is liquid (it barely compresses).
  - **Метастабільні стани** (metastable): the section bc is **пересичена пара** (supersaturated vapour); ef is **перегріта рідина** (superheated liquid).
  - The section cde is absolutely unstable (∂p/∂V > 0), so it can't be realized.
- **Бінодаль** (binodal) bounds the two-phase region; **спінодаль** (spinodal) bounds the unstable states.
- Above T_к: monotonic hyperbolas, so a gas **can't be liquefied by pressure alone** (CO₂ above 31 °C).
- ✏️ The main drawing: a family of isotherms T₁ < T_к < T₂, the wave and the horizontal line on T₁, the inflection point K, and the bell-shaped binodal with labelled regions "рідина", "рідина + пара", "газ".

## [D] Критичний стан (pp. 426–427)
- At T = T_к the three roots merge and the isotherm has an **inflection point K**.
  $$p_к = \frac a{27b^2},\qquad V_к = 3b,\qquad T_к = \frac{8a}{27Rb}\qquad(17.12)$$
- In the critical state the difference between liquid and gas disappears, and the compressibility → ∞.
- You can go from gas to liquid **without a phase transition** by going round the two-phase region via T > T_к (p. 430).

## [E] Порівняння з дослідом (pp. 430–432), short
- a and b depend on T. The **критичний коефіцієнт** $K_к = \dfrac{RT_к}{p_кV_к} = \dfrac83 \approx 2{,}67$ in theory, but for H₂ it is 3,12 and for water 4,46. In reality V_к ≈ 2b.
- **Зведене рівняння** (reduced equation), with π = p/p_к, ω = V/V_к, τ = T/T_к: $\left(\pi + \dfrac3{\omega^2}\right)(3\omega - 1) = 8\tau$ (17.18), the same for all substances. This is the **закон відповідних станів** (law of corresponding states).
