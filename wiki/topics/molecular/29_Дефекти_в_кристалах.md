## [A] Навіщо (p. 453)
- A perfect structure exists only in small volumes. Defects largely determine the **структурно-чутливі властивості** (structure-sensitive properties): electrical and thermal conductivity, strength…

## [B] Класифікація (p. 453) — the main table
| вимірність | тип | приклади |
|---|---|---|
| 0 (нульвимірні) | **точкові** (point): size ~ one or a few interatomic distances in all directions | vacancies (Schottky), Frenkel pairs, impurity atoms |
| 1 (одновимірні) | **лінійні** (line) | **дислокації**: edge and screw |
| 2 (двовимірні) | **поверхневі** (planar) | grain boundaries, mosaic blocks, stacking faults, twins, the surface |
| 3 (тривимірні) | **об'ємні** (volume) | cracks, inclusions, pores |

## [C] Точкові дефекти (pp. 453–455)
- **Atomic** ones:
  - **дефект за Шотткі** (Schottky defect): an empty node, a vacancy (рис. 18.5 а);
  - **дефект за Френкелем** (Frenkel defect): a vacancy **plus** a nearby interstitial atom (рис. 18.5 в). It needs a little less energy than a Schottky defect, 2–4 еВ in both cases;
  - **домішкові атоми** (impurity atoms): interstitial or substituting a host atom (рис. 18.5 б).
- Electronic ones (free electrons, holes, the **exciton** and the **polaron**, Pekar's theory) and energy ones (**phonons**, quanta of lattice vibration): mention them in one line.
- Equilibrium concentration of vacancies: $n_{в} = n_0e^{-W_{в}/kT}$ (18.4). It grows quickly with T; near melting n_в/n₀ ≈ 10⁻⁴. Compare Boltzmann's distribution on [17, 19](17,19_Розподіл_Больцмана_Максвелла-Больцмана.md).
- ✏️ Draw three small square grids: one with a missing atom, one with an atom moved from its node into a gap, one with a foreign atom.

## [D] Дислокації (pp. 455–456)
- **Крайова** (edge dislocation, рис. 18.6): one atomic plane ends inside the crystal, like a book with one page torn halfway.
- **Гвинтова** (screw dislocation, рис. 18.7): two half-planes slip by one period relative to each other.
- Dislocations can't end inside a crystal; they form loops or reach the surface.
- **Вектор Бюргерса** (Burgers vector): go round a contour by a whole number of steps. Around a dislocation the contour **doesn't close**, and the gap a′a″ is the Burgers vector, a measure of the lattice distortion.
- **Густина дислокацій** (dislocation density): the number of dislocation lines crossing a unit area.
- ✏️ Sketch the "extra half-plane" with a Burgers contour round it.

## [E] Поверхневі та об'ємні (p. 456)
- **Двійники** (twins): two crystals of one substance grown together along a crystallographic plane that is a mirror plane.
