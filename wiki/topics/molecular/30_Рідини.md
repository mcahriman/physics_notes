## [A] Місце рідин (pp. 462–463)
- The three states differ in how strong **the interaction of molecules is compared with their thermal motion**.
- Liquids sit **between gases and solids**. Molecules interact strongly, as in a solid, but sit on no periodic lattice.
- Frenkel's idea: a liquid is a "**disordered solid**", with **short-range order** and **no long-range order** ([28](28_Класифікація_кристалів.md)).

## [B] Радіальна функція розподілу (pp. 462–463)
- N(r) is the number of neighbours at a distance r from a chosen molecule.
  - Solid: sharp discrete peaks to large r (fixed positions).
  - Liquid: a few blurred maxima, damped towards a constant (short-range order).
  - Gas: smooth, with no structure.
- **Bernal's model**: the structure is described by the radii of the coordination spheres, the number of molecules in them, and how blurred they are.
- ✏️ Draw three small graphs N(r) side by side, as in рис. 19.1 а/б/в.

## [C] Тепловий рух (Френкель) (pp. 463–464)
- **Oscillation** about a temporary equilibrium (period about 10⁻¹² с), then a **jump** to a neighbouring position. These jumps are **трансляційні рухи** and need vacancies, or "holes".
- The **час осілого життя** (residence time): $\tau = Ae^{\Delta W/kT}$ (19.1), where ΔW is the potential barrier. It shrinks as T rises.
- The jumps explain **fluidity** and **diffusion** in liquids.

## [D] Властивості (pp. 464–466)
- They keep their **volume** (not their shape) and have a **free surface**.
- **Мала стисливість** (low compressibility): $\beta_T = -\dfrac1V\left(\dfrac{\partial V}{\partial p}\right)_T$ (19.2), about 10⁻⁹–10⁻¹⁰ Па⁻¹, tens to hundreds of thousands of times smaller than for gases. This is strong repulsion at work.
- **Теплове розширення** (thermal expansion): $\alpha_p = \dfrac1V\left(\dfrac{\partial V}{\partial T}\right)_p$ (19.3), usually > 0.
  - ⚡ **The water anomaly**: between 0 and 3,98 °C water **shrinks** when heated, so its density is greatest at 3,98 °C. The cause is its open, tetrahedral, hydrogen-bonded structure.
- Heat capacity is close to that of solids and much larger than that of gases, because heat goes into both kinetic and potential energy.

## [E] Перенос у рідинах (pp. 466–467) — contrast with gases ([23–25](23-25_Явища_переносу.md))
| | гази | рідини |
|---|---|---|
| механізм | free flights over ⟨l⟩ | jumps between equilibrium positions |
| D | ⅓⟨v⟩⟨l⟩; 0,1–0,9 см²/с | ⟨δ²⟩/(6τ) (19.4); 4–5 orders of magnitude smaller; $D = Be^{-\Delta W/kT}$ (19.5) |
| η від T | **grows** with T | **falls** with T: $\eta = Ae^{\Delta W/kT}$, **Frenkel's formula** (19.7) |
| λ від T | grows | falls roughly linearly |
- Water's viscosity falls by 20 % from 20 to 30 °C; glycerine's falls 2,4 times.
